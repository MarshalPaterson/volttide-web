ABOUT VOLTTIDE

Forged in the gritty damp basements of the underground circuit, VOLTTIDE fuses high-octane rockabilly twang with aggressive 77-style punk rock and menacing psychobilly shock-horror energy.

Driven by hyper-slapped upright bass, heavy distortion, frantic beat patterns, and tongue-in-cheek lyrics about haunted wards, bad prescriptions, and midnight hot-rod runs, VOLTTIDE delivers a chaotic live show that leaves stages vibrating.

"If Dick Dale, The Cramps, and The Living End got locked in an insane asylum together — VOLTTIDE is the sound of them breaking out."
